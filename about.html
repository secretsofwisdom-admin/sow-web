About Secrets of Wisdom · NZ

Guided by the Flow of Heaven and Earth

Every life moves to a rhythm written long before we arrive — in the turn of the seasons, the pull of a place, the moment of a first breath. Secrets of Wisdom · NZ was born to help you hear that rhythm again. My practice draws on four classical arts — Feng Shui, BaZi (the Four Pillars of Destiny), Qi Men, and Tarot — each a different lens onto the same timeless connection between Heaven, Earth, and Humanity.

In this field, I go by NZ. I didn't come to these arts as a believer chasing wonder — I came as an observer and a quiet skeptic. For as long as I can remember, I've watched and researched, turning the same questions over: why did this happen to this person, and could it have been met or lived through in a healthier, more harmonious way? I never went looking for the mysterious; if anything, the mystery came looking for me. I still need things to make sense — a logical thread I can follow — and it's that same grounded, questioning eye I bring to every chart, every space, and every reading.

These traditions speak in different tongues, and together they tell a fuller story. Feng Shui reads the energy of the spaces you live in; BaZi maps the elemental blueprint you were born with; Qi Men reveals the strategy hidden in a single moment; and Tarot speaks in image and archetype, surfacing the quiet emotional currents running beneath a question. My work is to listen across all of them — translating the silent language of energy into insight you can actually use.

Every consultation is a collaboration. Whether we're aligning your home, decoding your destiny chart, choosing an auspicious moment, or drawing a Tarot spread for a question that's been weighing on you, the aim is the same: clarity where there was fog, and a sense of moving with the current rather than against it. Wherever you are on your path, the wisdom is here — and it's waiting.
