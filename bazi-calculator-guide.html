The BaZi Calculator: What It Shows and How to Use It

November 16, 2026

The BaZi calculator on this site is free and needs no registration. Enter a date, time and place of birth, and it builds the chart of the Four Pillars of Destiny: the pillars of the year, month, day and hour, each with its Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch.

What the chart shows

Under every Earthly Branch you see its hidden stems, with the main qi set larger. The calculator names the Ten Gods for the chart and lists its symbolic stars; choosing a star or a Ten God opens a panel with its description. The Five Elements of the chart are shown alongside.

Luck pillars and years

Below the natal chart stands the row of luck pillars, the ten-year periods of a life, with the age at which each begins. Opening a decade shows its annual pillars, so you can see which year falls into which period.

A transit chart for any date

Next to the natal chart the calculator builds a second chart for any moment you choose: the luck pillar, year, month and day of that date. The notes beside it list the clashes, combinations, harms and punishments inside the natal chart and between the natal chart and the chosen date. Click any stem or branch to see the relations it takes part in.

Details for an exact calculation

If the hour of birth is unknown, the chart can be built without it. The advanced settings include true solar time and the choice of how the late Rat hour is counted.

The Chinese calendar

The same page opens a Chinese calendar: the pillar of every day, the twelve day officers, lunar dates, solar terms, Moon phases and the Flying Stars of the year and the month.

Saving charts

With a free account you can save charts to your library, sort them into folders and open any of them again in one click. The calculator works in English and in Russian.
