The Natal Chart Calculator: Wheel, Houses and Aspects

November 16, 2026

The natal chart calculator on this site is free and needs no registration. Enter a date, time and place of birth, and it draws the chart wheel of Western astrology for that moment.

What the chart shows

The wheel shows the zodiac signs, the house cusps and the positions of the Sun, the Moon and the planets. A summary names the Ascendant, the Sun sign and the Moon sign, and a table lists every placement with its sign, degree and house.

Houses and aspects

You can choose the house system used for the chart. The aspects between the planets are listed next to the wheel.

When the time of birth is unknown

If the time of birth is unknown, tick the box for an unknown time and the chart is built from the date and the place alone.

Reading the wheel

The wheel can be enlarged with the buttons beside it. The calculator follows the light and dark themes of the site and works in English and in Russian.
