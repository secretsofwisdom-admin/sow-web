Solar & Lunar Eclipse Corridor · February–March 2026

Friends, on February 17th an annular solar eclipse and Chinese New Year arrived at the same time — a truly rare coincidence, especially since we've entered the Year of the Fire Horse, which comes only once every 60 years. The energy right now is very powerful, fiery, swift. Strong fire moves fast — it sweeps away the old and clears the path for the new. And it's important to meet this wave with a clear heart and a clear mind.

Right now we are in what's known as the eclipse corridor — it will last until March 3rd, when a total lunar eclipse takes place. Interestingly, March 3rd is also the Lantern Festival, the culmination of Chinese New Year celebrations. Everything comes full circle.

This window is the best time for inner work. For cleansing, for letting go of what has been holding you back, and for planting intentions for the entire year.

What you can do during these days:

🌑 During the solar eclipse period — work on clarity. Ask yourself honest questions: who am I without my usual roles and masks? What is truly calling from my heart? Write down your intentions — not as a list of goals, but as states of being. Peace, freedom, love, abundance.

🌕 Closer to March 3rd — focus on cleansing. Take a salt bath, visualizing the water washing away everything that no longer serves you. Bring order to your home — physical space is directly connected to your inner space. Release old resentments, karmic knots, heavy stories.

After March 3rd the corridor will close, and the space will begin to solidify whatever state you are in. Everything planted during these days will be growing throughout all of 2026. And this year promises to be a very powerful one.

If in recent days you've been feeling pressure, anxiety, a strange inner trembling — don't be afraid. It's normal. Your soul is sensing the scale of the changes ahead. The Fire Horse is not carrying us to a comfort zone — it's carrying us toward what is truly ours.

Don't fear the shadow. It is where the brightest stars are born ✨
