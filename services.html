Feng Shui Consultation

Classical space harmonisation — aligning your environment with the flow of Qi for clarity, prosperity, and ease.

Feng shui is an ancient Chinese Taoist art and philosophy — one that has guided humanity for thousands of years. Its name translates literally as "wind and water," symbols of health and flow. At its heart, feng shui is the study of Qi: the vital life energy that moves through every space, every object, and every living being. The practice seeks to invite Sheng Qi — the auspicious, nourishing energy — while gently dissolving Sha Qi, the stagnant and disruptive forces that quietly drain us.

The balance of Yin and Yang — rest and movement, stillness and vitality — lies at the heart of every consultation. When these forces are in harmony within your home, you feel it: better sleep, clearer thinking, a sense of ease that is hard to explain but impossible to ignore.

This is not simply about rearranging furniture. It is about understanding the invisible forces that shape your everyday experience — and consciously choosing to work with them. At Secrets of Wisdom, every consultation is deeply personalised, drawing on classical Flying Star analysis, compass readings, and elemental balance to meet you exactly where you are.

Whether you are moving into a new home, navigating a life transition, or simply longing for a space that finally feels like you — the wisdom is here, waiting.

Chinese Astrology Natal Chart Reading

Decode your elemental blueprint and life cycles through the ancient system of Four Pillars of Destiny.

BaZi — the Four Pillars of Destiny — is the cornerstone of Chinese astrology. It maps the precise cosmic energies present at the moment of your birth: the year, month, day, and hour, each represented by a Heavenly Stem and Earthly Branch. Together, these pillars form a blueprint of your elemental nature.

Your chart reveals far more than personality. It illuminates your strengths and vulnerabilities, the rhythm of your luck cycles, and the timing of pivotal life transitions — career shifts, relationships, health patterns, and opportunities for growth. Understanding your BaZi is like holding a map of the terrain ahead.

Every reading is a personal dialogue. I walk you through your Day Master, elemental balance, the ten-year luck pillars unfolding across your lifetime, and the current annual and monthly influences shaping your present moment. You leave with clarity, direction, and a deeper understanding of the forces at work in your life.

Qi Men Oracle

Direct strategic guidance through the ancient cosmic divination system of Qi Men Dun Jia.

Qi Men Dun Jia is one of the most powerful and sophisticated divination systems in Chinese metaphysics. Unlike natal astrology, which maps your life blueprint, Qi Men answers specific questions in real time. Should I take this opportunity? What is the true nature of this situation? Each reading is concise and direct. You bring a clear question, and the oracle responds with strategic insight — not vague predictions, but actionable clarity rooted in the language of heaven, earth, and the human realm.

Date Selection

Align your most important events with cosmic timing that supports success, ease, and lasting resonance.

Not every day carries the same energy. Some days are naturally aligned with beginnings — with movement, growth, and positive momentum. Others carry friction, instability, or hidden obstacles that make even well-planned efforts harder than they need to be.

Date Selection is the practice of identifying which days are genuinely favourable for what you are planning — and which ones to avoid. Using classical Chinese timing systems, I calculate the energetic quality of specific dates within your chosen window and match them to the nature of your event.

Whether you are launching a business, signing a contract, scheduling surgery, moving into a new home, planning a wedding, or booking an important journey — the timing you choose matters more than most people realise. The right date does not guarantee success, but it removes unnecessary resistance and puts the natural flow of energy behind your intention.

You bring the event and your available window. I find the dates that work in your favour.
